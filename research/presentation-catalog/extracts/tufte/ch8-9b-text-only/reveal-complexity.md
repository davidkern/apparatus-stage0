**Name:** Reveal-Complexity
**Definition:** Give visual access to the subtle and difficult—the revelation of complex patterns that would otherwise remain hidden.

**How Tufte articulates it (Epilogue):**
> "Design is choice. The theory of the visual display of quantitative information consists of principles that generate design options and that guide choices among options."

> "What is to be sought in designs for the display of information is the clear portrayal of complexity. Not the complication of the simple; rather the task of the designer is to give visual access to the subtle and the difficult—that is, the revelation of the complex."

**Examples referenced:**
- None specific; capstone statement

**Framework category:** Explanation / Aggregative (summarizing complex phenomena)

**Source type:** Explicit prose (epilogue)

**Notes:** This is Tufte's culminating statement of purpose for data graphics. It distinguishes "complication of the simple" (chartjunk, overdesign) from "revelation of the complex" (genuine insight). This is perhaps the meta-intent underlying all visualization.
