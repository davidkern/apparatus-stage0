name: Elaborate Causal Variable
---

**Definition**: Provide detailed resolution on the independent/causal variable (typically on horizontal axis) to show its workings in detail.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "Many graphics plot, in essence, effect [on y-axis] vs. cause [on x-axis] and a longer horizontal helps to elaborate the workings of the causal variable in more detail." (p. 187)

**Examples from text**:
- The cause-effect diagram (p. 187) showing horizontal axis labeled "cause" and vertical labeled "effect"

**Framework category**: **Relational** — Causal elaboration shows the relationship between cause and effect more completely.
