name: Use Thin Lines (Minimize Line Weight)
---

**Definition**: Employ thin, light lines in data graphics to create elegance and avoid visual heaviness.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "Lines in data graphics should be thin. One reason eighteenth- and nineteenth-century graphics look so good is that they were engraved on copper plates, with a characteristic hair-thin line. The drafting pens of twentieth-century mechanical drawing thickened linework, making it clumsy and unattractive." (p. 185)

**Examples from text**:
- Copper plate engravings of 18th-19th century as positive models
- Contrast with 20th century mechanical drafting pen graphics

**Framework category**: **Aesthetic Coherence** — Line weight is a formal property affecting overall visual quality.
