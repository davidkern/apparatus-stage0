name: Reveal Complexity (Not Complicate Simplicity)
---

**Definition**: Use graphics to make complex data comprehensible rather than to inflate simple data into appearing complex.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "What is to be sought in designs for the display of information is the clear portrayal of complexity. Not the complication of the simple; rather the task of the designer is to give visual access to the subtle and the difficult—that is, the revelation of the complex." (Epilogue)

**Examples from text**:
- This is the culminating statement of the entire book
- The contrast with "puffed-up display of a small data set" (p. 184) as anti-example

**Framework category**: **Evidential** — Revealing complexity is about truthful representation of data's actual richness.
