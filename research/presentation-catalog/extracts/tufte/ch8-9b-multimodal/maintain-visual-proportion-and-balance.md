name: Maintain Visual Proportion and Balance
---

**Definition**: Create harmonious relationships among graphical elements (lines, text, shading) so they form a visually coherent whole.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "Graphical elements look better together when their relative proportions are in balance. An integrated quality, an appropriate visual linkage between the various elements, results." (p. 184)

**Examples from text**:
- Stockhausen's musical score "Zyklus" as positive example of visual balance (p. 184)
- Counter-example: "Seats and Votes in 1968" graph described as "heavy handed, with nearly every element out of balance: the clotted ink, the poor style of lettering, the puffed-up display of a small data set, the coarse texture of the entire graphic, and the mismatch between drawing and surrounding text" (p. 184)

**Framework category**: New category: **Aesthetic Coherence** — The graphic functions as a unified visual composition.
