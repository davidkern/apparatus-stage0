These intents were directly articulated in Tufte's prose or explicitly stated principles:

1. **Integrate Text and Data** — "Data graphics are paragraphs about data and should be treated as such"
2. **Label Directly on Graphic** — "write little messages on the plotting field"
3. **Make Complexity Accessible** — Section title and friendly graphic discussion
4. **Minimize Encoding Burden** — Friendly/unfriendly contrast
5. **Respect Reading Direction** — Left-to-right reading guidance
6. **Guide Attention Appropriately** — Exploration vs. communication caveat
7. **Maintain Visual Proportion** — "Graphical elements look better together when their relative proportions are in balance"
8. **Use Thin Lines** — "Lines in data graphics should be thin"
9. **Differentiate by Line Weight** — "contrast in line weight represents contrast in meaning"
10. **Favor Horizontal Orientation** — "Graphics should tend toward the horizontal"
11. **Let Data Shape Determine Graphic Shape** — "If the nature of the data suggests the shape..."
12. **Reveal Complexity** — Epilogue culminating statement
13. **Apply Principles Flexibly** — "Design is choice"
14. **Design for Typography Legibility** — Friendly/unfriendly typography contrast
15. **Design for Inclusive Accessibility** — Color-deficiency guidance
