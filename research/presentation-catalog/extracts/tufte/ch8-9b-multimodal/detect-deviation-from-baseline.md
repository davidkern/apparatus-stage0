name: Detect Deviation from Baseline
---

**Definition**: Enable perception of deviations from a reference line or baseline, particularly in time-series displays.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Through the horizon analogy and shading suggestion.

**Examples from text**:
- "Our eye is naturally practiced in detecting deviations from the horizon, and graphic design should take advantage of this fact. Horizontally stretched time-series are more accessible to the eye" (p. 186)
- Shaded area chart showing filled region below time-series line: "a shaded, high contrast display might occasionally be better than the floating snake" (p. 187)

**Framework category**: **Comparative** — Deviation detection is comparison against a baseline reference.
