name: Let Data Shape Determine Graphic Shape
---

**Definition**: Allow the nature of the data itself to suggest the proportions of the graphic rather than imposing arbitrary aspect ratios.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "If the nature of the data suggests the shape of the graphic, follow that suggestion." (p. 190)

**Examples from text**:
- Playfair's exceptions to horizontal orientation: "skyrocketing government debt displays" that warranted vertical proportion (p. 188)
- Golden Rectangle discussion noting that context-dependence matters: "viewer responses were found to be highly context-dependent" (p. 190)

**Framework category**: **Evidential** — Data integrity includes letting data characteristics drive form.
