**Name:** Data-Built Data Measures

**Definition:** The purpose of constructing the graphical marks that plot data out of the data values themselves (numbers as marks), increasing information density.

**How Tufte articulates it:** Quoting John Tukey: "If we are going to make a mark, it may as well be a meaningful one. The simplest--and most useful--meaningful mark is a digit." (p. 140)

**Specific examples:**
- Stem-and-leaf plot: "constructs the distribution of a variable with numbers themselves" - volcano heights displayed with digits forming histogram shape (p. 140)
- Living histogram: college students arranged by height form bimodal distribution (photograph, p. 140)
- WWI divisions chart by Colonel Ayres: division numbers form time-series (p. 141)

**Framework category:** **Distributional** - distribution shape emerges from data values themselves
