**Name:** Triple-Functioning Temporal Display

**Definition:** The purpose of showing three aspects of temporal data in a single graphic: count at each time point, identity of elements, and duration of each element's presence.

**How Tufte articulates it:** Describing Ayres' WWI graphic: "The triple-functioning data measure shows: (1) the number of divisions in France for each month, June 1917 to October 1918; (2) what particular divisions were in France in each month; and (3) the duration of each division's presence in France." (p. 141)

**Specific examples:**
- Division number chart: stacked division numbers form cumulative time-series
- Each division number appears in every month it was present, showing duration
- Specific division identity readable at each time point
- "Constructing the data measures out of each American division's name... turns what might have been a routine time-series into an elegant display"

**Framework category:** **Temporal** - multi-aspect temporal relationship display
