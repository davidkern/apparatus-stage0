These intents were directly articulated by Tufte in text:

1. **Drawing Economy for Exploration** - "research worker's time should be devoted to matters other than drawing lines"
2. **Chart Frame Mobilization** - "frame can become an effective data-communicating element simply by erasing part of it"
3. **White Grid Coordinate Display** - "white grid... shows the coordinate lines more precisely than ticks alone"
4. **Joint Distribution Display** - "Dot-dash-plots make routine what good data analysts do already"
5. **Multi-Plot Continuity** - "quantitative history of a single observation can be traced"
6. **Historical Context in Reference Frame** - "historical high and low... excellent practice"
7. **Self-Explanation without Legend** - "does not require any viewing or decoding instructions"
8. **Audience Capability Assumption** - "why not assume that if you understand it, most other readers will, too?"
9. **Graphical Element Multifunctionality** - "same ink should often serve more than one graphical purpose"
10. **Data-Built Data Measures** - Tukey quote on meaningful marks
11. **Triple-Functioning Temporal Display** - explicit enumeration of the three functions
12. **Graphical Puzzle Avoidance** - "danger... graphical puzzles"
13. **Design Efficiency Beyond Information** - "single dimension of a complex and multivariate design task"
