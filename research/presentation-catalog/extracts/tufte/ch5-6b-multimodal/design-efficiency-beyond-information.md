**Name:** Design Efficiency Beyond Information

**Definition:** The purpose of acknowledging that information density is one of several design considerations, not an absolute maximum to pursue without limit.

**How Tufte articulates it:** "Maximizing data ink (within reason) is but a single dimension of a complex and multivariate design task... There remain, however, many other considerations in the design of statistical graphics--not only of efficiency, but also of complexity, structure, density, and even beauty." (p. 137)

**Specific examples:**
- "Within reason" qualification repeated throughout
- Aesthetic consideration: "a thin baseline looks good" (p. 128)
- Complexity, structure, density, beauty named as additional dimensions
- The principle "helps conduct experiments in graphical design. Some of those experiments will succeed."

**Framework category:** **Evidential** - acknowledges limits of any single optimization principle
