**Name:** White Grid Coordinate Display

**Definition:** The purpose of using negative space (erasure through the data measures) to indicate coordinate reference lines, showing grid positions more precisely than traditional tick marks.

**How Tufte articulates it:** "Even part of the data measures can be erased, making a white grid, which shows the coordinate lines more precisely than ticks alone." (p. 127)

**Specific examples:**
- Bar chart with white horizontal stripes cutting through bars at 5%, 10%, 15% levels (pp. 127-128)
- "The white grid eliminates the tick marks, since the numerical labels on the vertical are tied directly to the white lines" (p. 128)
- Notched box plot redesign uses same technique (p. 129)

**Framework category:** **Comparative** - white grid enables precise comparison across values against reference lines
