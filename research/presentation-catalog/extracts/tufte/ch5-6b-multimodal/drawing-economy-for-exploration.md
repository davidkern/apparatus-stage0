**Name:** Drawing Economy for Exploration

**Definition:** The purpose of minimizing production effort in graphics to enable rapid, iterative data exploration where the researcher's time should be devoted to analysis rather than rendering.

**How Tufte articulates it:** "The large reduction in the amount of drawing is relevant for the use of such designs in informal, exploratory data analysis, where the research worker's time should be devoted to matters other than drawing lines." (p. 125)

**Specific examples:**
- Parallel schematic plot: original requires 80 separate straightedge placings (50 horizontals, 30 verticals)
- Erased version requires only 10 verticals to show same information
- The visual comparison shows dramatically simplified box plot forms using only range lines and median dots

**Framework category:** **Instructional** - procedural concern about workflow efficiency in analysis practice
