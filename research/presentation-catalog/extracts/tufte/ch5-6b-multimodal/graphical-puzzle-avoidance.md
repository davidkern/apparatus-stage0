**Name:** Graphical Puzzle Avoidance

**Definition:** The purpose of ensuring that sophisticated encoding schemes remain interpretable rather than becoming cryptic puzzles decipherable only by their creator.

**How Tufte articulates it:** "The danger of multifunctioning elements is that they tend to generate graphical puzzles, with encodings that can only be broken by their inventor. Thus design techniques for enhancing graphical clarity in the face of complexity must be developed along with multifunctioning elements." (p. 138)

**Specific examples:**
- Warning that multifunctionality has limits
- Range-frame succeeds because interpretation is natural
- Dot-dash-plot approaches the boundary of puzzle complexity
- "Mobilize every graphical element, perhaps several times over, to show the data" tempered by clarity requirement

**Framework category:** **Instructional** - balances density with interpretability
