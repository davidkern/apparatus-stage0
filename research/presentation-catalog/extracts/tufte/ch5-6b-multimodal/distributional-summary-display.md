**Name:** Distributional Summary Display

**Definition:** The purpose of communicating the shape and spread of data through compact visual summaries that convey quartiles, medians, ranges, and outliers.

**How Tufte articulates it:** Demonstrated through progressive box plot redesigns that maintain statistical information while reducing ink. The erased parallel schematic plot shows "the same information" with minimal marks.

**Specific examples:**
- Erased box plots: vertical lines encode range, dots encode median (p. 125)
- Variable width notched box plot showing telephone bills vs. years in Chicago (p. 129)
- Quartile plot derived from range-frame: "ten extra numbers (the minimum, maximum, two quartiles, and the median for both variables)" (p. 132)

**Framework category:** **Distributional** - directly addresses shape/spread communication
