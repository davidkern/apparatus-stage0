**Name:** Graphical Element Multifunctionality

**Definition:** The purpose of having single graphical marks or elements carry multiple pieces of information simultaneously, increasing data density.

**How Tufte articulates it:** "The same ink should often serve more than one graphical purpose. A graphical element may carry data information and also perform a design function usually left to non-data-ink. Or it might show several different pieces of data." (p. 138)

**Specific examples:**
- Blot map: "simultaneously locates the geographic unit on a two-dimensional surface, describes the shape of the geographic unit, and indicates the level of the variable displayed by color or intensity of shading" (p. 138)
- Range-frame: frame line encodes both plot boundary AND data extent
- Dot-dash marginals: dashes serve as both axis decoration AND marginal distribution

**Framework category:** **Aggregative** - efficiency through multiple encoding
