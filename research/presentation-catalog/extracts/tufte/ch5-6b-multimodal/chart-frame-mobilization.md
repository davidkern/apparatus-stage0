**Name:** Chart Frame Mobilization

**Definition:** The purpose of transforming decorative or structural graphical elements (axes, frames, grids) into data-carrying elements that communicate quantitative information.

**How Tufte articulates it:** "The frame of a graphic can become an effective data-communicating element simply by erasing part of it. The frame lines should extend only to the measured limits of the data rather than, as is customary, to some arbitrary point like the next round number." (p. 130)

**Specific examples:**
- Range-frame: "explicitly shows the maximum and minimum of both variables plotted (along with the range)" (p. 130)
- Quartile plot: "small shift in the remaining ink turns each range-frame into a quartile plot" (p. 132)
- Dot-dash-plot: "entire frame can be turned into data by framing the bivariate scatter with the marginal distribution of each variable" (p. 133)

**Framework category:** **Aggregative** - transforms structural elements into summary statistics carriers
