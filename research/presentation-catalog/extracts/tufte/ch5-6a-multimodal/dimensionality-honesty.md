**Name:** Dimensionality Honesty

**Definition:** The purpose of using only the spatial dimensions genuinely required to represent data, avoiding fake perspective or unnecessary dimensionality that distorts perception.

**How Tufte articulates it:** "The addition of a fake perspective to the data structure clutters many graphics. This variety of chartjunk, now at high fashion in the world of Boutique Data Graphics, abounds in corporate annual reports, the phony statistical studies presented in advertisements, the mass media, and the more muddled sorts of social science research." (p. 118)

**Specific examples:**
- 3D College Enrollment chart (p. 118): "This may well be the worst graphic ever to find its way into print"
- The chart uses five colors and 3D surfaces to show what are essentially five percentage values per year
- California irrigation water graphic (p. 119): elaborate 3D waffle charts on a map

**Framework category:** **Distributional** - false dimensionality distorts perception of distributional shape and magnitude
