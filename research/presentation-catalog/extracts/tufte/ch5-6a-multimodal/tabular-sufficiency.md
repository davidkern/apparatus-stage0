**Name:** Tabular Sufficiency

**Definition:** The purpose of recognizing when data is best communicated through a table rather than a graphic, choosing the appropriate form for the content.

**How Tufte articulates it:** "Since the information consists of a few numbers and a good many words, it is best to pass up the computerized graphics capability this time and tell the story with a table" (p. 120)

**Specific examples:**
- Political science chart (p. 120) replaced with prose table (p. 121): "Content and tone of front-page articles in 94 U.S. newspapers"
- The table presents the same data more clearly with proper category descriptions
- "The observed values of the percentages should be printed instead" of encoding in bar heights

**Framework category:** **Aggregative** - choosing the right format for efficient summary presentation
