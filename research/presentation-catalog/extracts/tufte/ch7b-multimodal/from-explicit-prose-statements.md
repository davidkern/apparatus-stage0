These intents were directly articulated by Tufte in text:

1. **Puzzle Avoidance** - "A sure sign of a puzzle is that the graphic must be interpreted through a verbal rather than a visual process"
2. **Natural Visual Ordering** - "the mind's eye does not readily give a visual ordering to colors... varying shades of gray show varying quantities better than color"
3. **Information Flow Organization** - "graphical methods that organize and order the flow of graphical information presented to the eye"
4. **Multiple Viewing Depths** - three depths explicitly enumerated
5. **Multiple Visual Angles** - "Each separate line of sight should remain unchanging"
6. **Data Integration with Separation** - "integrated through its connected content, separated in that the eye follows several different and uncluttered paths"
7. **Data Density Maximization** - formula provided and discussed
8. **Viewing Architecture Analysis** - "analysis of the viewing architecture of a graphic"
