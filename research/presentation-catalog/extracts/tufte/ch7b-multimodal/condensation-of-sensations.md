**Name:** Condensation of Sensations

**Definition:** The purpose of achieving integrated, unified visual experience from complex data, analogous to artistic composition where elements cohere into a whole.

**How Tufte articulates it:** Through the Matisse quotation that opens the chapter break: "I want to reach that state of condensation of sensations which constitutes a picture." (p. 160)

**Specific examples:**
- The Matisse quote serves as epigraph connecting data graphics to visual art
- The population density map achieves this: individual dots aggregate into corridors, landforms, patterns
- The slope graph achieves this: individual country changes cohere into overall "upward pattern"

**Framework category:** **Aggregative** - the synthetic goal of summarization into coherent whole
