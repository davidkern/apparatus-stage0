These intents were demonstrated through the figures:

1. **Rank Change Communication** - The slope graph (p. 158) visually demonstrates countries' changing positions through connecting lines with proportionally spaced endpoints
2. **Slope Deviation Detection** - Looking at the slope graph, Britain's declining rank visually "stands out" from upward-trending majority
3. **Multiple Viewing Depths** - The Census population density map (pp. 156-157) visually demonstrates all three viewing depths: overall East-West density gradient at distance, individual dots and corridors up close, 400,000-point grid implicitly
4. **Data Integration with Separation** - The arrow diagram (p. 159) visually shows three independent reading paths
5. **Anti-Sparsity** - The participation rate bar chart (p. 163) visually demonstrates wasted space: enormous gray bars encoding just 3 numbers
6. **Perceptual Capacity Respect** - The steel products table excerpt (p. 161) visually demonstrates readable high-density tabular data
