**Name:** Slope Deviation Detection

**Definition:** The purpose of enabling rapid identification of data elements that behave differently from the overall pattern through slope comparison.

**How Tufte articulates it:** "The slopes are also compared by reading down the collection of lines, and lines of unusual slope stand out from the overall upward pattern." (p. 159)

**Specific examples:**
- In the government receipts graphic, Britain's line notably diverges (moving down in rank)
- Most lines slope upward (increasing government receipts), making any flatter or descending line visually salient
- The parallel alignment of slopes creates a visual baseline for detecting anomalies

**Framework category:** **Comparative** - identifies deviation from expected pattern
