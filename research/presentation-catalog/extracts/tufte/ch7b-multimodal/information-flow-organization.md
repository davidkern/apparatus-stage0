**Name:** Information Flow Organization

**Definition:** The purpose of using graphical architecture to promote ordered, sequenced, hierarchical flow of information from the graphic to the viewer's perception.

**How Tufte articulates it:** "Central to maintaining clarity in the face of the complex are graphical methods that organize and order the flow of graphical information presented to the eye." He asks: "How can graphical architecture promote the ordered, sequenced, hierarchical flow of information from the graphic to the mind's eye?" (p. 154)

**Specific examples:**
- The shades of gray "provide an easily comprehended order to the data measures"
- The population density map reveals layered information: corridors connecting urban complexes, effects of landforms, clusters along rivers, small towns along highways "linked like a string of pearls"

**Framework category:** **Relational** - establishes hierarchy and connections within data
