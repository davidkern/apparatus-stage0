---

**Definition**: Make exceptional observations visually salient so they can be examined or investigated.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Through the "wildshot" observation example and hot spot detection.

**Examples from text**:
- Point A in scatterplot "hides in the marginal distribution but shows up as clearly exceptional in the bivariate scatter" (p. 14)
- Cancer map "unusual hot spots, including northern Minnesota and a few counties in Iowa and Nebraska" (p. 16)
- "the unusual experience of a county warrants further investigation" (p. 16)

**Framework category**: **Distributional** — Outliers are part of distribution shape; also **Evidential** — anomalies as evidence requiring explanation.
