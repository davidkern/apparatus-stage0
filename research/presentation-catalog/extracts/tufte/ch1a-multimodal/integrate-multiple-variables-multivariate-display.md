name: Integrate Multiple Variables (Multivariate Display)
---

**Definition**: Show relationships among multiple variables simultaneously.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "Excellence, nearly always of a multivariate sort" (p. 15)

**Examples from text**:
- Voyager 2 display: three radio bands, magnetic orientation, distance, and date all on one graphic (p. 29)
- Marey's train schedule: time, space, speed, stops all encoded (p. 31)
- Cancer maps encoding: rate, statistical significance, geography, gender, cancer type (pp. 17-19)

**Framework category**: **Relational** — Multivariate displays show relationships among variables.
