name: Support Multi-Level Reading (Overview to Detail)
---

**Definition**: Enable viewers to perceive both broad patterns and fine-grained details within the same graphic.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Graphics should "reveal the data at several levels of detail, from a broad overview to the fine structure" (p. 13)

**Examples from text**:
- Cancer maps allowing examination "ranging from the contemplation of general overall patterns to the detection of very fine county-by-county detail" (p. 16)
- Specific observations at multiple scales: national patterns (northeast cancer concentration), regional (Louisiana shipyard workers), and individual counties (Salem County, NJ bladder cancer) (pp. 16-20)

**Framework category**: **Distributional** — Shows shape/spread at multiple granularities; could also be a new category "Scalar" or "Hierarchical Detail"
