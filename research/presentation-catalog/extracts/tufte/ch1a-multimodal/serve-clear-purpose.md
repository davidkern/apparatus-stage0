name: Serve Clear Purpose
---

**Definition**: Have an explicit communicative function rather than existing as decoration or demonstration of technique.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Graphics should "serve a reasonably clear purpose: description, exploration, tabulation, or decoration" (p. 13)

**Examples from text**:
- This is a meta-intent about intentionality itself
- Anti-example: solar radiation/stock prices chart serving no legitimate purpose (p. 15)

**Framework category**: This is a meta-principle about intents rather than an intent itself.
