**Definition**: Form a memorable, retainable understanding that persists after viewing.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Through Playfair's argument for graphics over tables.

**Examples from text**:
- Playfair: "On inspecting any one of these Charts attentively, a sufficiently distinct impression will be made, to remain unimpaired for a considerable time, and the idea which does remain will be simple and complete" (p. 32)
- Contrast with tables: "a man who has carefully investigated a printed table, finds... that he has only a very faint and partial idea of what he has read; and that like a figure imprinted on sand, is soon totally erased" (p. 32)

**Framework category**: New category suggestion: **Mnemonic** or **Retention** — Purpose is cognitive persistence.
