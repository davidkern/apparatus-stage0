These intents were directly articulated in Tufte's bulleted list (p. 13) or explicit prose:

1. **Reveal Data** — "Graphics reveal data"
2. **Enable Comparison** — "encourage the eye to compare"
3. **Communicate Density** — "present many numbers in a small space"
4. **Support Multi-Level Reading** — "reveal data at several levels of detail"
5. **Make Large Datasets Coherent** — "make large data sets coherent"
6. **Direct Attention to Substance** — "induce the viewer to think about the substance"
7. **Preserve Data Integrity** — "avoid distorting what the data have to say"
8. **Serve Clear Purpose** — "serve a reasonably clear purpose"
9. **Integrate with Text/Statistics** — "be closely integrated with statistical and verbal descriptions"
