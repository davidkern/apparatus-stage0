name: Communicate Direction and Flow
---

**Definition**: Show movement, direction, or quantity of flow between locations.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Through Halley's wind map and Minard's export flows.

**Examples from text**:
- Halley's trade winds map: "the sharp end of each little stroak pointing out that part of the Horizon, from whence the wind continually comes" (p. 23)
- Minard's wine export map giving "quantity as well as direction to the data measures" (p. 24)

**Framework category**: **Relational** — Flow implies relationship between origin and destination; also **Temporal** when showing movement over time.
