**Definition**: Suggest causal relationships, anomalies, or patterns worth investigating further.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "The maps provide many leads into the causes—and avoidance—of cancer" (p. 16)

**Examples from text**:
- Louisiana cancer rates suggesting occupational exposure from shipyard asbestos work (p. 16)
- North-central stomach cancer suggesting Scandinavian smoked fish consumption (p. 16)
- Salem County bladder cancer leading to investigation of chemical industry workers (p. 16)
- Galaxy map clusters prompting questions about galactic structure (p. 26)

**Framework category**: **Relational** — Suggests causal/correlational relationships; could also be new category **Exploratory** or **Hypothesis-Generating**
