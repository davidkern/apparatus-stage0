**Definition**: Work in concert with verbal descriptions and statistical analysis rather than standing in isolation.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Graphics should "be closely integrated with the statistical and verbal descriptions of a data set" (p. 13)

**Examples from text**:
- Cancer atlas maps accompanied by statistical analysis and textual interpretation
- Anscombe's quartet paired with both the data table and statistical summary (pp. 13-14)

**Framework category**: **Instructional** or new category **Contextual** — The graphic is part of a larger communicative system.
