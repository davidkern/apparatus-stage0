**Definition**: Show how quickly quantities change over time or space.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Through Marey's train schedule design.

**Examples from text**:
- Marey's Paris-Lyon train schedule: "The slope of the line reflects the speed of the train: the more nearly vertical the line, the faster the train" (p. 31)
- The TGV overlay showing dramatic speed improvement over 100 years (p. 31)

**Framework category**: **Temporal** — Rate is derivative of temporal change.
