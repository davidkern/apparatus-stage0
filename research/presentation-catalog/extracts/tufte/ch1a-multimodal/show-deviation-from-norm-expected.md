name: Show Deviation from Norm/Expected

**Definition**: Display how actual values deviate from baseline, expected, or normal values.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Through examples showing comparison to baselines.

**Examples from text**:
- NYC weather chart: "daily high and low temperatures are shown in relation to the long-run average" (p. 30)
- Cancer maps using statistical significance relative to U.S. average (legend: "Not significantly different from U.S. as a whole" vs. "Significantly higher/lower") (p. 16)

**Framework category**: **Comparative** — Deviation is comparison against a reference.
