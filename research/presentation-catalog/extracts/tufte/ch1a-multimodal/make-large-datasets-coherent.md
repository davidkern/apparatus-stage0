name: Make Large Datasets Coherent
---

**Definition**: Organize and structure complex data so the viewer can comprehend relationships and patterns that would be incomprehensible in raw form.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Graphics should "make large data sets coherent" (p. 13)

**Examples from text**:
- Cancer atlas organizing 3,056 counties' worth of mortality data into interpretable geographic patterns
- Galaxy map making sense of 1.3 million galaxy positions
- The entire chapter's emphasis on graphics that "successfully organizes a large collection of numbers" (p. 30)

**Framework category**: **Aggregative** — Creating coherence from multiplicity is a form of meaningful aggregation.
