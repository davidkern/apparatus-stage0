name: Scale Consistency (Show Data Variation, Not Design Variation)
---

**Definition**: Maintain uniform scales and visual encoding throughout a graphic so that all variation the viewer perceives represents actual data variation.

**How Tufte articulates it**:
- "Show data variation, not design variation." (p. 61)
- "The confounding of design variation with data variation over the surface of a graphic leads to ambiguity and deception, for the eye may mix up changes in the design with changes in the data." (p. 61)

**Examples from these pages**:
- Nobel Prize graphic using irregular time scale (7 decades at 10 years, final period at 4 years) creating false decline (p. 60)
- OPEC oil prices graphic with 5 different vertical scales and 2 different horizontal scales, making same quantity appear 15.1x larger depending on position (p. 61)

**Framework category**: **Temporal** (when involving time) / **Comparative** - Consistent scales enable valid temporal progression and cross-point comparison.
