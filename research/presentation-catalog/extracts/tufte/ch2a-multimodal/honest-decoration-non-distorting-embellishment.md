name: Honest Decoration (Non-Distorting Embellishment)
---

**Definition**: When decoration is used, ensure it does not distort the data measures while potentially editorializing about substance.

**How Tufte articulates it**:
- "It is easy enough to decorate these data without lying" (p. 58)
- "Sometimes decoration can help editorialize about the substance of the graphic. But it is wrong to distort the data measures—the ink locating values of numbers—in order to make an editorial comment or fit a decorative scheme." (p. 59)

**Examples from these pages**:
- Tufte's elaborately decorated fuel economy graphic with ornamental frame (p. 59) - "many decorations but no lies"
- Contrast with the "road perspective" fuel economy graphic where decoration causes distortion

**Framework category**: This may require a new category: **Aesthetic** or could be considered a constraint on **Instructional** - decoration should instruct/editorialize without misleading.
