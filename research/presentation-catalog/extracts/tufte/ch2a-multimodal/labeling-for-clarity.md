---

**Definition**: Use thorough labeling to defeat ambiguity and help viewers correctly interpret the data.

**How Tufte articulates it**:
- "Clear, detailed, and thorough labeling should be used to defeat graphical distortion and ambiguity. Write out explanations of the data on the graphic itself. Label important events in the data." (p. 56)

**Examples from these pages**:
- Playfair's national debt chart labeling historical events: "Revolution," "Accession of Queen Ann," "Beginning of the Spanish War," "End of Continental War," etc. (pp. 64-65)
- Business Week's oil price graphic labeling "Crude oil price adjusted for inflation" (p. 63)

**Framework category**: **Instructional** - Labels guide interpretation and instruct the viewer on how to read the graphic.
