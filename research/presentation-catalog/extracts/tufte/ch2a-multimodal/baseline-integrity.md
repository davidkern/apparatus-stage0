**Definition**: Maintain honest baselines that do not hide or obscure data, particularly negative values or the zero point.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Demonstrated through negative example:
- "the case of the disappearing baseline... a negative income in 1970, which is disguised by having the bars begin at the bottom at approximately minus $4,200,000" (p. 54)

**Examples from these pages**:
- Day Mines annual report hiding 1970 loss with false baseline (p. 54)

**Framework category**: **Evidential** - Baseline integrity is a constraint on preserving evidential honesty.
