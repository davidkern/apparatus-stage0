name: Reasoning Support (Graphics as Analytical Instruments)

**Definition**: Enable viewers to use graphics as instruments for reasoning about quantitative information, not merely as decoration.

**How Tufte articulates it**:
- Reference to John Tukey: "graphics were used as instruments for reasoning about quantitative information" (p. 53)
- "The special power of graphics comes in the display of large data sets." (p. 56)
- Contrast with "graphics had to be 'alive,' 'communicatively dynamic,' overdecorated and exaggerated" (p. 53)

**Examples from these pages**:
- Tufte's corrected fuel economy chart with connected data points enabling analysis of rate changes
- The Economist's multi-series comparison of nominal price, real price, and energy/GDP ratio

**Framework category**: **Aggregative** / **Relational** - Reasoning support aggregates data for comprehension and reveals relationships.
