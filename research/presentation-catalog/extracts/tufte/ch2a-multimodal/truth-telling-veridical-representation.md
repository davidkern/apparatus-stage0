**Definition**: Communicate quantitative information accurately so viewers can learn the truth from the data.

**How Tufte articulates it**:
- "Graphical excellence begins with telling the truth about the data." (p. 53)
- "A graphic does not distort if the visual representation of the data is consistent with the numerical representation." (p. 55)

**Examples from these pages**:
- The Day Mines annual report hiding negative income by using a false baseline (p. 54)
- The airline commission payments graphic comparing 6 months to full years (p. 54)
- The Pittsburgh insane asylum cost comparison treating ordinal as cardinal data (p. 55)

**Framework category**: **Evidential** - This is about establishing and maintaining trust in the evidence presented.
