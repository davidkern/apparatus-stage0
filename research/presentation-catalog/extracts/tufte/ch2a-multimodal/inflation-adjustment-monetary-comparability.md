name: Inflation Adjustment (Monetary Comparability)
---

**Definition**: When showing monetary values over time, adjust for inflation to enable meaningful comparison of real value.

**How Tufte articulates it**:
- "The only way to think clearly about money over time is to make comparisons using inflation-adjusted units of money." (p. 63)
- Playfair: "This is in real and not in nominal millions" (p. 65)

**Examples from these pages**:
- Multiple oil price graphics showing nominal dollars, missing that real price had declined 1975-1978 (p. 63)
- Business Week, Sunday Times (London), and The Economist correctly showing real vs. nominal prices (p. 63)
- Playfair's 1786 alternative chart showing national debt interest "in real and not in nominal millions" (p. 65)

**Framework category**: **Temporal** - This is specifically about maintaining comparability across time when monetary units are involved.
