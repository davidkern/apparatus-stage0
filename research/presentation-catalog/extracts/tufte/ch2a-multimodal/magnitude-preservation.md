name: Magnitude Preservation

**Definition**: Represent numerical magnitude, not just order, when data has cardinal (not merely ordinal) properties.

**How Tufte articulates it**:
- "And sometimes the fact that numbers have a magnitude as well as an order is simply forgotten" (p. 55)

**Examples from these pages**:
- Pittsburgh insane asylum costs graphic showing $147, $172, $198, $213, $214 with building images of similar visual size, treating cardinal data as if merely ordinal (p. 55)

**Framework category**: **Comparative** / **Distributional** - Magnitude preservation enables valid comparison and understanding of distribution.
