name: Integrate with Verbal/Statistical Description
---

**Definition:** Function as part of a larger explanatory apparatus, working alongside text and numerical summaries rather than standing alone.

**How Tufte articulates it:** Graphics should "be closely integrated with the statistical and verbal descriptions of a data set."

**Examples referenced:**
- The chapter itself demonstrates this by combining prose descriptions with referenced graphics
- Playfair's atlas described as combining graphical with textual presentation

**Framework category:** **Instructional** / **Explanation** - the intent is about how graphics fit into broader communication contexts.
