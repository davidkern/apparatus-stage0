name: A1. Decoration Without Purpose
---

**Definition:** Graphics that serve aesthetic or attention-getting purposes without serving data communication.

**How Tufte articulates it:** Lists "decoration" as a possible but lesser purpose. Implies criticism of graphics that don't "show the data."

**Framework category:** Anti-intent / violation of Evidential integrity.
