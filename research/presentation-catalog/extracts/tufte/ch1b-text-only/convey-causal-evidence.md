---

**Definition**: Present data in a way that moves beyond description toward causal explanation — showing not just that something happened, but implying or demonstrating why.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "The problem with time-series is that the simple passage of time is not a good explanatory variable: descriptive chronology is not causal explanation." Graphics should be "moved toward causal explanation by smuggling additional variables into the graphic design."

**Examples referenced**:
- U.S. House mail volume peaking before elections (time-series with clear causal mechanism)
- Retail sales decomposition showing seasonal, trading-day, and trend components
- Lung cancer vs. cigarette consumption scatterplot

**Framework mapping**: **Evidential** — specifically causal evidence. This extends beyond simple claim+evidence to implying or establishing causation.
