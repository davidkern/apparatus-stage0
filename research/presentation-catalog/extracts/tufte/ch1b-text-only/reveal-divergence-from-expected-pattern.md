name: REVEAL DIVERGENCE FROM EXPECTED PATTERN
---

**Definition**: Show where empirical observations deviate from theoretical expectations or where different measurements disagree.

**How Tufte articulates it**: In discussing Phillips curves: "demonstrating the collapse of what was once thought to be an inverse relationship between the variables." And for copper conductivity: "The very different answers reported in the published literature result mainly from impurities in the samples."

**Examples referenced**:
- Phillips curves showing breakdown of expected inverse relationship
- Thermal conductivity measurements showing inter-laboratory variation

**Framework mapping**: **Evidential** — specifically showing disconfirming or conflicting evidence. Related to Comparative but with an expectation baseline.
