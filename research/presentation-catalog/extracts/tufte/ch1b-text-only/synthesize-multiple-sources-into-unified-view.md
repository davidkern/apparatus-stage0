---

**Definition**: Bring together findings from many separate studies or measurements into a single display that enables comparison and assessment.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Regarding the thermal conductivity graphic: "Note how effectively the graphic organizes a vast amount of data, recording findings of hundreds of studies on a single page and, at the same time, enforcing comparisons of the varying results."

**Examples referenced**:
- Thermal conductivity of copper (measurements from many labs on one plot)

**Framework mapping**: **Aggregative** — specifically meta-analytic aggregation. Combines evidence from multiple sources into a single view.
