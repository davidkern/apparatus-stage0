name: Belligerent Multivariate Design
---

**Tufte's statement**: "Occasionally graphics are belligerently multivariate, advertising the technique rather than the data."

**Implied intent**: Multivariate integration should be "gentle and unobtrusive" — technique should serve data, not showcase itself.
