The text extraction contains significant OCR noise, particularly in:
- Figure captions and labels (garbled text in image regions)
- Non-English text (Minard's French original)
- Tabular data within figures

These artifacts made some passages difficult to interpret but did not significantly impact extraction of major intents, which came from Tufte's prose commentary.
