name: ENABLE RAPID COMPARISON VIA CONSISTENT DESIGN (Small Multiples)
---

**Definition**: Use repeated identical graphical structures to allow viewers to focus on differences in data rather than differences in design, enabling efficient comparison across many cases.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "Small multiples are economical: once viewers understand the design of one slice, they have immediate access to the data in all the other slices. Thus, as the eye moves from one slice to the next, the constancy of the design allows the viewer to focus on changes in the data rather than on changes in graphical design."

**Examples referenced**:
- Los Angeles air pollution display (12 time-space-pollutant slices)
- Phillips curve plots for nine countries

**Framework mapping**: **Comparative** — but as a meta-pattern for presentation form. Could also be considered an **Aggregative** intent when the goal is to show a pattern across many instances.
