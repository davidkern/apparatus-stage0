name: SHOW BEFORE-AND-AFTER STATE CHANGE
---

**Definition**: Contrast the state of a system before and after a significant event or intervention, making the change visually evident.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "Finally, a vivid design (with appropriate data) is the before-after time-series."

**Examples referenced**:
- Cabrera's candidate magnetic monopole signal (flux before and after detection event)
- Minard's bridge collapse on the Rhone (before/after 1840)

**Framework mapping**: **Comparative** — specifically temporal comparison of states. Overlaps with Temporal but the emphasis is on the contrast created by an event.
