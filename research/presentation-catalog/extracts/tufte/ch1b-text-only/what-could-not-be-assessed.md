name: What Could Not Be Assessed
---

1. **Visual effectiveness**: Without seeing the graphics, cannot evaluate whether the described intents are actually achieved through the visual form.

2. **Design specifics**: References to line thickness, shading, slope directions, and spatial arrangements cannot be verified.

3. **Anti-pattern identification**: Would likely find more anti-patterns if the "bad" examples were visible.

4. **Subtlety of integration**: Tufte emphasizes that good multivariate graphics are "gentle and unobtrusive" — this quality cannot be assessed without viewing.

5. **Completeness of intent list**: Graphics often communicate intents that their creators don't explicitly articulate. The unseen graphics may embody additional intents not described in the prose.
