name: REVEAL COMPARISON WITHIN SELF OVER TIME
---

**Definition**: Show how the same phenomenon changes across different time periods, enabling viewers to compare a variable with its own historical values.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Via Playfair's critique of the bar chart: "it does not compare the same branch of commerce with itself at different periods." Time-series are superior because they "imprint upon the mind that distinct idea" through the "dimension that is formed by duration."

**Examples referenced**:
- Playfair's first bar chart (Scotland imports/exports 1781) — noted as inferior precisely because it lacks temporal self-comparison
- Playfair's 250-year wheat prices chart comparing prices to wages over time

**Framework mapping**: **Temporal** — but specifically self-referential temporal comparison. Related to existing Comparative category but with a temporal axis.
