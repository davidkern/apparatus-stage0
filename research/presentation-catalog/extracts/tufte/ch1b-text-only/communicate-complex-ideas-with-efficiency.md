name: COMMUNICATE COMPLEX IDEAS WITH EFFICIENCY
---

**Definition**: Maximize the ratio of ideas conveyed to resources expended (time, space, cognitive effort).

**How Tufte articulates it**: "Graphical excellence is that which gives to the viewer the greatest number of ideas in the shortest time with the least ink in the smallest space." And: "Graphical excellence consists of complex ideas communicated with clarity, precision, and efficiency."

**Examples referenced**:
- The mail workload graphic is "worth at least 700 words" — the number used in the accompanying news article
- Minard's graphic tells "a rich, coherent story with its multivariate data, far more enlightening than just a single number bouncing along over time"

**Framework mapping**: This is a **meta-principle** rather than a specific intent category. It governs how all intents should be realized.
