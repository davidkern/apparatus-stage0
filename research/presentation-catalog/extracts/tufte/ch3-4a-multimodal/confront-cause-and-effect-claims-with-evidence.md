name: Confront Cause-and-Effect Claims with Evidence
---

**Definition**: Provide visual grounds for evaluating causal assertions, making it possible to test whether claimed relationships hold in the data.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Relational graphics "confront statements about cause and effect with evidence" (p. 82)

**Examples from text**:
- The "Views on the Economy Influence Carter Support" graphic (p. 81): Attempts to show economic perception's effect on voting, though Tufte criticizes its execution
- Walking speed vs. population study: The graphic provides evidence for the hypothesis that city size affects pace of life

**Framework category**: **Evidential** — The graphic serves as evidence against which claims can be tested.
