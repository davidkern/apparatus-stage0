name: Avoid Decoration Without Data
---

**Definition**: Ensure graphic elements serve data communication rather than mere ornamentation or attention-getting.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Critiques the "doctrine of boring data" that leads to "an Art Department and an Art Director... required to animate the data" (p. 80). "As the art bureaucracy grows, style replaces content." (p. 80)

**Examples from text**:
- The cafeteria pictograph using repeated human figures to show 90% — pure decoration (p. 81)
- Art director quote: graphics "intended more to lure the reader's attention away from the advertising than to explain the news" (p. 80)
- Critique of "Plain statistics embellished or humanized with pictures" (p. 80)

**Framework category**: **Evidential** — Decoration without data undermines evidential function. Also relates to Data-Ink concept from Chapter 4.
