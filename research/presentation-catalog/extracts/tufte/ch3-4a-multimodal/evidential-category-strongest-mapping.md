These chapters heavily emphasize the evidential function of graphics. The core argument is that graphics should serve as instruments of evidence and reasoning, not decoration or attention-getting. Key articulations:

- "confront statements about cause and effect with evidence" (relational graphics as tests of claims)
- "Above all else show the data" (evidence primacy)
- "instruments to help people reason about quantitative information" (graphics as cognitive tools)
- Data-ink concept (ink should carry information)
