name: Anti-Pattern: Elaborate Non-Data Detail
---

**What it reveals**: Non-data elements should be minimized.

**Tufte's description**: Playfair's early charts "devoted too much of their ink to graphical apparatus, with elaborate grid lines and detailed labels" (p. 91)

**Violated intent**: Maximize Data-Ink Ratio
