These intents were directly articulated in Tufte's prose:

1. **Explain Relationships** — "confront statements about cause and effect with evidence" (p. 82)
2. **Explain Data** — "an explanatory graphic" (p. 84)
3. **Respect Audience Intelligence** — E.B. White quote and application (p. 81)
4. **Find and Present the Right Numbers** — "If the statistics are boring, then you've got the wrong numbers" (p. 80)
5. **Show the Data** — "Above all else show the data" (p. 92)
6. **Maximize Data-Ink Ratio** — Formal definition and formula (p. 93)
7. **Help People Reason** — "instruments to help people reason about quantitative information" (p. 91)
8. **Draw Attention to Sense and Substance** — "draw the viewer's attention to the sense and substance" (p. 91)
9. **Integrate Three Expertises** — "substantive, statistical, and artistic" (p. 87)
10. **Communicate Tremendous Power** — "tremendous communicative power of graphics" (p. 87)
