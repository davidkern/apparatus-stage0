**Definition**: Serve as cognitive instruments that facilitate analytical thinking about numbers.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "Essentially statistical graphics are instruments to help people reason about quantitative information." (p. 91)

**Examples from text**:
- Contrast with "graphics worthy of the Museum of Modern Art" — art appreciation is not the goal
- The entire Chapter 4 introduction frames graphics as reasoning tools

**Framework category**: **Instructional** or **Evidential** — Graphics enable the cognitive work of understanding evidence.
