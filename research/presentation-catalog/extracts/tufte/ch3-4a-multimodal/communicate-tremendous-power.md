**Definition**: Leverage the unique capacity of graphics to convey quantitative observation more effectively than words.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "It wastes the tremendous communicative power of graphics to use them merely to decorate a few numbers. Moreover, much of the world these days is observed and assessed quantitatively—and well-designed graphics are far more effective than words in showing such observations." (p. 87)

**Examples from text**:
- This is articulated as the waste involved in poor graphics rather than demonstrated by example

**Framework category**: **Evidential** — The communicative power exists specifically for conveying evidence.
