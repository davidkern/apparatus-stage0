Weaker presence, but the "reasoning instrument" framing places graphics in the instructional family — they help audiences learn something about the world.

---

## Potential New Intent Categories Suggested

1. **Epistemic Respect**: Treating audience as capable reasoners deserving sophisticated presentation.

2. **Production Quality**: Meta-level concern with how graphics should be made (three expertises).

3. **Anti-Decoration**: Explicit rejection of ornament that doesn't carry data.

4. **Explanatory vs. Descriptive**: Distinction between graphics that show what happened (time-series) and graphics that show why (relational).
