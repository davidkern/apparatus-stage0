name: Find and Present the Right Numbers
---

**Definition**: Ensure the data being visualized are the substantively meaningful numbers that reveal the actual story, not just convenient or decorative data.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "If the statistics are boring, then you've got the wrong numbers. Finding the right numbers requires as much specialized skill—statistical skill—and hard work as creating a beautiful design or covering a complex news story." (p. 80)

**Examples from text**:
- Counter-examples: News graphics that miss "the real news actually in the data" (p. 87)
- Anti-pattern: graphics that "lure the reader's attention away from the advertising" rather than "explain the news in any detail" (p. 80)

**Framework category**: New category suggestion: **Substantive Selection** or **Evidential** — the graphic communicates only insofar as it presents the right evidence.
