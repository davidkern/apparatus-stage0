name: Anti-Pattern: Bivariate Data Forced into Univariate Design
---

**What it reveals**: Form should match data structure.

**Tufte's description**: "In seeking to avoid the subtleties of the scatterplot, artists drew up these convoluted specimens, forcing bivariate data into a univariate design" (p. 81)

**Violated intent**: Explain Relationships, Achieve Graphical Sophistication

**Visual example**: The "Comparative Tax Loads and Economic Growth Rates" chart (p. 81) and the "Views on the Economy Influence Carter Support" paired bar charts — both represent two-variable relationships but refuse to use two-dimensional plotting.
