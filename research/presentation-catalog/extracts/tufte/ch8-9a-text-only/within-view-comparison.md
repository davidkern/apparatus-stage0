**Name:** Within-View Comparison

**Definition:** Enable the eye to make comparisons without requiring memory or page-turning by presenting related data elements within a single visual field.

**Category Mapping:** Comparative (difference assessment)

**How Tufte Articulates It:**
- "High-density graphics help us to compare parts of the data by displaying much information within the view of the eye"
- "We look at one page at a time and the more on the page, the more effective and comparative our eye can be"
- References x-ray analysis advice to "search a reduced image so that the whole display can be perceived on at least one occasion without large eye movement"

**Examples Referenced:**
- Bertin's 17 small-scale graphics on single page
- Consumer Reports frequency-of-repair matrix

**Source Type:** Explicit prose with citation to perceptual research

**Limitations from Text-Only:** Cannot assess visual span or eye movement requirements of examples.
