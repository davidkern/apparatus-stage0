**Name:** Multivariate Revelation

**Definition:** Simultaneously display relationships among multiple variables, revealing patterns that emerge only when several dimensions are viewed together.

**Category Mapping:** Relational (connection between multiple dimensions)

**How Tufte Articulates It:**
- Small multiples are "deftly multivariate"
- They show "shifts in the relationship between variables as the index variable changes (thereby revealing interaction or multiplicative effects)"

**Examples Referenced:**
- Consumer Reports table mixing manufacturer, car type, year, and trouble spots
- Chromosome comparison across species
- Melanoma study with location, gender, and count

**Source Type:** Explicit prose

**Limitations from Text-Only:** Cannot observe how multiple variables are visually encoded simultaneously.
