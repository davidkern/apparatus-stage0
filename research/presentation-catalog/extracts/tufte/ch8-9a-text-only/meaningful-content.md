**Name:** Meaningful Content

**Definition:** Graphics should address matters of importance and significance, deriving power from the weight of their subject matter.

**Category Mapping:** Meta-intent (content significance)

**How Tufte Articulates It:**
- "Visually attractive graphics also gather power from content and interpretations beyond the immediate display of some numbers"
- "The best graphics are about the useful and important, about life and death, about the universe"

**Examples Referenced:**
- Minard's depiction of Napoleon's Russian campaign losses
- Galaxy map

**Source Type:** Explicit prose

**Limitations from Text-Only:** N/A - this is an argument about content selection.
