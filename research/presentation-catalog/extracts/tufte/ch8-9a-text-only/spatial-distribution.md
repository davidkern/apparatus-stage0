**Name:** Spatial Distribution

**Definition:** Show how phenomena are distributed across geographic or anatomical space, revealing patterns of concentration, absence, or variation.

**Category Mapping:** Distributional (shape/spread in space)

**How Tufte Articulates It:**
- Implied through examples rather than explicit principle
- Maps described as achieving 100,000-250,000 bits per square inch
- "Perhaps some day statistical graphics will perform as successfully as maps in carrying information"

**Examples Referenced:**
- Map of 30,000 French communes
- Melanoma distribution on human body
- Los Angeles pollution spatial distribution
- Galaxy map with location encoding

**Source Type:** Inferred from examples

**Limitations from Text-Only:** Cannot observe spatial encoding or distribution patterns directly.
