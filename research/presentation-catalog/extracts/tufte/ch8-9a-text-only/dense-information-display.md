**Name:** Dense Information Display

**Definition:** Communicate large quantities of data efficiently within limited visual space, enabling comprehensive understanding that cannot be achieved through text or tables alone.

**Category Mapping:** Aggregative (summary presentation of complex data)

**How Tufte Articulates It:**
- "More information is better than less information, especially when the marginal costs of handling and interpreting additional information are low"
- "The simple things belong in tables or in the text; graphics can give a sense of large and complex data sets that cannot be managed in any other way"
- "Maximize data density and the size of the data matrix, within reason"

**Examples Referenced:**
- Sunshine record: ~1,000 numbers per square inch
- Map of French communes: ~9,000 numbers per square inch
- Galaxy map: 110,000 numbers per square inch (cited as "current record")
- Comparison table of data density across publications (Nature, Science, WSJ, etc.)
