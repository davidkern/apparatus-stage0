**Name:** Credibility Through Completeness

**Definition:** Establish trustworthiness of the presentation by showing comprehensive data rather than selective summaries, allowing the audience to verify claims against full evidence.

**Category Mapping:** Evidential (confidence and provenance)

**How Tufte Articulates It:**
- "Data-rich designs give a context and credibility to statistical evidence"
- "Low-information designs are suspect: what is left out, what is hidden, why are we shown so little?"
- "Summary graphics can emerge from high-information displays, but there is nowhere to go if we begin with a low-information design"

**Examples Referenced:**
- Contrast between high-density scientific graphics and low-density news graphics
- Comparison of Nature (48 numbers/sq in median) vs Pravda (0.2 numbers/sq in)

**Source Type:** Explicit prose

**Limitations from Text-Only:** Cannot compare visual impression of "complete" vs "sparse" presentations.
