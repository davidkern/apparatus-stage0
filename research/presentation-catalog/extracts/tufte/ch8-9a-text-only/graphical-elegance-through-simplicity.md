**Name:** Graphical Elegance

**Definition:** Achieve visual quality through simplicity of design combined with complexity of data, avoiding decoration that does not serve information.

**Category Mapping:** Meta-intent (design quality principle)

**How Tufte Articulates It:**
- "Graphical elegance is often found in simplicity of design and complexity of data"
- "For non-data-ink, less is more. For data-ink, less is a bore."
- "Beautiful graphics do not traffic with the trivial"
- Graphics should "avoid content-free decoration, including chartjunk"

**Examples Referenced:**
- Minard's campaign graphics
- Playfair and Marey's work
- Galaxy map as example of modern quality work

**Source Type:** Explicit prose

**Limitations from Text-Only:** Cannot observe visual elegance directly; must rely on Tufte's characterization.
