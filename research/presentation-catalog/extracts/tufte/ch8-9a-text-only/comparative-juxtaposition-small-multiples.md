**Name:** Comparative Juxtaposition

**Definition:** Enable comparison across a changing variable by presenting multiple instances of the same graphic structure, indexed by changes in another dimension (time, category, condition).

**Category Mapping:** Comparative (showing difference across conditions)

**How Tufte Articulates It:**
- "Small multiples resemble the frames of a movie: a series of graphics, showing the same combination of variables, indexed by changes in another variable"
- "The design remains constant through all the frames, so that attention is devoted entirely to shifts in the data"
- Well-designed small multiples are "inevitably comparative"

**Examples Referenced:**
- Los Angeles air pollution over 23 hours (video frames)
- Melanoma distribution on body (with male/female comparison)
- Sampling error distributions (12 samples of 50 random normal deviates)
- Herring age composition 1908-1913 (tracking 1904 spawn cohort)
- Chromosomes of man, chimpanzee, gorilla, orangutan
- Consumer Reports frequency-of-repair records (1976-1981)

**Source Type:** Explicit prose with extensive examples

**Limitations from Text-Only:** Cannot observe how visual constancy enables comparison or assess the effectiveness of the multivariate display.
