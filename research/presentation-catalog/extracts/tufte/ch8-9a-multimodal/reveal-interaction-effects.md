name: Reveal Interaction Effects

**Definition**: Show how the relationship between variables changes as a third (or fourth) variable changes, revealing multiplicative or interaction effects.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Small multiples are "often narrative in content, showing shifts in the relationship between variables as the index variable changes (thereby revealing interaction or multiplicative effects)" (p. 175)

**Examples from text**:
- Herring age composition showing how the dominant 1904 cohort appears at different ages in successive years
- Melanoma distribution showing gender-specific patterns (women: more on face; men: more on scalp/ears)

**Framework category**: **Relational** — Interaction effects are a form of complex relationship among multiple variables.
