name: Perform Data Graphical Arithmetic
---

**Definition**: Use graphic composition to show addition, subtraction, or decomposition of data — the whole equals the sum of its parts.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Notes "data graphical arithmetic, similar to that of the multiwindow plot" (p. 171) in reference to melanoma distribution.

**Examples from text**:
- Melanoma map: total distribution = male distribution + female distribution (p. 171)
- The three-panel structure showing combined, then gender-separated views

**Framework category**: **Comparative** / **Aggregative** — Shows how parts compose into wholes.
