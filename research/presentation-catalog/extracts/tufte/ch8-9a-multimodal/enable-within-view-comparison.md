name: Enable Within-View Comparison

**Definition**: Display enough information within a single visual field that the eye can compare parts of the data without requiring memory or page-turning.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "High-density graphics help us to compare parts of the data by displaying much information within the view of the eye: we look at one page at a time and the more on the page, the more effective and comparative our eye can be" (p. 168)

**Examples from text**:
- Reference to x-ray analysis: "search a reduced image so that the whole display can be perceived on at least one occasion without large eye movement" (p. 168)
- Small multiples enabling comparison across all frames simultaneously

**Framework category**: **Comparative** — The intent is to enable comparison; density is the mechanism.
