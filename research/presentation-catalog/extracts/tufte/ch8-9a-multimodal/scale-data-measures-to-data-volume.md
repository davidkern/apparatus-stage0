name: Scale Data Measures to Data Volume
---

**Definition**: Adjust the visual size of data marks as data volume increases — smaller dots for dense scatters, thinner lines for busy time-series.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "As the volume of data increases, data measures must shrink (smaller dots for scatters, thinner lines for busy time-series)" (p. 168)

**Examples from text**:
- Implicit in the discussion of high-information graphics requiring special design care
- The galaxy map using tiny marks to represent 2+ million encoded rectangles

**Framework category**: This is a design technique rather than a communicative intent per se.
