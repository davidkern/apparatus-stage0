name: Show Change Over an Index Variable (Small Multiples)
---

**Definition**: Display a series of graphics showing the same variables indexed by changes in another variable, enabling perception of how relationships shift.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "Small multiples resemble the frames of a movie: a series of graphics, showing the same combination of variables, indexed by changes in another variable" (p. 170)

**Examples from text**:
- Los Angeles air pollution: 23 hourly frames showing hydrocarbon emission distribution (p. 170)
- Melanoma distribution on head/neck, split by gender (p. 171)
- Herring catch age composition 1908-1913, showing 1904 cohort aging through catches (p. 172)
- Chromosome comparison across man, chimpanzee, gorilla, orangutan (p. 172-173)
- Consumer Reports repair records across years and trouble spots (p. 174)

**Framework category**: **Temporal** (when index is time) or **Comparative** (when index is category) — Small multiples serve both purposes depending on the indexing variable.
