name: Engage Significant Subject Matter
---

**Definition**: Apply graphical excellence to topics that matter — life, death, the universe — rather than trivial subjects.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "The best graphics are about the useful and important, about life and death, about the universe. Beautiful graphics do not traffic with the trivial" (p. 177)

**Examples from text**:
- Minard's graphics of army deaths in Russian campaign
- Cancer mortality maps
- Galaxy distribution mapping cosmic structure

**Framework category**: This is a meta-principle about topic selection rather than a specific communicative intent.
