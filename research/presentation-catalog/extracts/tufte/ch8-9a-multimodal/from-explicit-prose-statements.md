These intents were directly articulated in Tufte's prose:

1. **Maximize Data Density** — "Maximize data density and the size of the data matrix, within reason"
2. **Enable Within-View Comparison** — "displaying much information within the view of the eye"
3. **Establish Credibility Through Data Richness** — "Data-rich designs give a context and credibility"
4. **Shrink Graphics** — "Graphics can be shrunk way down"
5. **Show Change Over an Index Variable** — "a series of graphics... indexed by changes in another variable"
6. **Maintain Design Constancy** — "design remains constant through all the frames"
7. **Reveal Interaction Effects** — "revealing interaction or multiplicative effects"
8. **Create Narrative Through Sequence** — "often narrative in content"
9. **Achieve Elegance** — "simplicity of design and complexity of data"
10. **Engage Significant Subject Matter** — "the best graphics are about life and death"
11. **Facilitate Comparison Through Format Choice** — "sentence is a poor way to show more than two numbers"
12. **Order Data Meaningfully** — "better sequences than alphabetical"
13. **Integrate Words, Numbers, and Drawing** — explicit in list of attractive display qualities
14. **Display Accessible Complexity** — explicit in list of attractive display qualities
