name: Establish Credibility Through Data Richness
---

**Definition**: Use comprehensive data display to establish trust and transparency, as sparse displays raise suspicions about what has been omitted.

**How Tufte articulates it**: "Data-rich designs give a context and credibility to statistical evidence. Low-information designs are suspect: what is left out, what is hidden, why are we shown so little?" (p. 168)

**Examples from text**:
- The implicit contrast between high-density scientific graphics (Nature, Journal of Royal Statistical Society) and low-density publications
- The argument that "more information is better than less information" (p. 168)

**Framework category**: **Evidential** — Data richness serves evidential integrity by demonstrating nothing is hidden.
