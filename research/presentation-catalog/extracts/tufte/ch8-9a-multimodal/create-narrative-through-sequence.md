name: Create Narrative Through Sequence
---

**Definition**: Use ordered sequence of graphics to tell a story about change, causation, or development.

**How Tufte articulates it**: Small multiples are "often narrative in content" (p. 175)

**Examples from text**:
- Herring catches telling the story of the 1904 cohort as it ages from 4-year-olds to 9-year-olds
- LA air pollution showing the daily buildup and dissipation of emissions
- The implicit narrative of Consumer Reports showing reliability trends over time

**Framework category**: **Temporal** — Narrative is inherently temporal, unfolding through sequence.
