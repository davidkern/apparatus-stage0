**Definition**: The event that disrupts equilibrium and launches the narrative.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to recognize the moment
when everything changes—the point of no return that initiates the main action.
Also called catalyst, story spark, or triggering event.

**Examples**:
- "Then the security breach was discovered."
- "On October 15, the production database crashed."
- The moment when a character receives a call, letter, or visitor that
  changes everything.
- The discovery of a bug that leads to a redesign.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Event (specific)
- Contained by: Story, Narrative Arc

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Distinct from Causation (which
explains why). Inciting Incident marks the moment, emphasizing disruption
rather than mechanism.
