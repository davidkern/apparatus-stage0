name: In Medias Res
---

**Definition**: Beginning in the middle of action, with context supplied
later.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants immediate engagement, trusting the
audience to catch up as the narrative provides context retrospectively.

**Examples**:
- "The server was on fire. Three engineers hunched over terminals. Nobody
  knew who had pushed the change."
- Openings that drop the audience into crisis.
- Cold opens in television.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Present Crisis, Deferred Context
- Contained by: Story

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Hook but structural
rather than content-based. It's a strategy, not a moment.
