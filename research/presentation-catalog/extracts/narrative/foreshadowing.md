name: Foreshadowing
---

**Definition**: Planting hints about future events.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to receive advance
signals (conscious or unconscious) that prepare them for later developments,
creating coherence and retrospective satisfaction.

**Examples**:
- "The error logs would prove significant later."
- Chekhov's gun—details that will matter.
- Ominous warnings that prove accurate.
- Setup for future payoff.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Hint, Warning (subtle)
- Contained by: Scene, Exposition

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Warning but covert
rather than explicit. Foreshadowing doesn't announce itself.
