**Definition**: Establishing the baseline situation before disruption.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to understand the "normal
world" against which subsequent events will be measured. This creates the
contrast necessary for change to be meaningful.

**Examples**:
- "In the beginning, the codebase was clean and well-documented."
- Opening chapters that establish character routines and setting.
- The "Before" state in a case study.
- Establishing shots in film that show ordinary life.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Setting, Character Introduction, Status
- Contained by: Story, Narrative Arc

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to State/Status but with
temporal and narrative function—it establishes what will change.
