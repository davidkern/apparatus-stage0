**Definition**: Presenting opposition to the protagonist's goals.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to understand what
stands in the way—the forces creating conflict and obstacle.

**Examples**:
- "The legacy system resisted every change."
- Villain introductions and motivations.
- Competing teams or priorities.
- Technical debt as adversary.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Opposition, Obstacle, Threat
- Contained by: Story, Conflict

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: No direct analog. Creates the
tension that Emphasis/Warning responds to.
