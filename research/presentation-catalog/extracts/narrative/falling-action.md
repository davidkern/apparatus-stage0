name: Falling Action
---

**Definition**: The aftermath of the climax as consequences unfold.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to see how the decisive
moment plays out—what happens after the turning point.

**Examples**:
- "With the patch deployed, we watched the metrics stabilize."
- Characters dealing with the results of their decisions.
- The gradual restoration of order.
- Verification and validation after a fix.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Consequence, Aftermath
- Contained by: Story, Narrative Arc

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Causation (showing
effects) but framed as resolution rather than explanation.
