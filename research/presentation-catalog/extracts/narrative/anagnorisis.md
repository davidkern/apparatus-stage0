**Definition**: Recognition of true identity or situation, typically with
tragic or dramatic implications.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to experience the
moment when a character discovers something fundamental about who they
are or what they've done—often with irreversible consequences.

**Examples**:
- Oedipus discovering he killed his father.
- Realizing the bug you've been chasing was in your own code.
- The moment of understanding that changes everything.
- Discovery that a trusted system was compromised all along.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Identity, Truth, Consequence
- Contained by: Climax, Scene

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: A dramatic form of Epiphany with
darker implications. Related to Error acknowledgment but personal and
transformative.
