**Definition**: A traditional phrase signaling that a story is beginning.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author/performer wants to shift the audience
into story-receiving mode—marking the boundary between ordinary discourse
and narrative space.

**Examples**:
- "Once upon a time..."
- "A long time ago, in a galaxy far, far away..."
- "Coyote was going there."
- Traditional tale openings in any culture.

**Containment**:
- Contains: (nothing—it's a boundary marker)
- Contained by: Story (at start)

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: A narrative form of Greeting/Marker.
It signals mode shift rather than just section boundary.
