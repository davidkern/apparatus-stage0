**Definition**: A sudden realization that transforms understanding.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to experience a
moment of insight alongside the character—seeing something that was
always there but suddenly becomes clear.

**Examples**:
- "Suddenly, it clicked: the race condition wasn't in our code—it was
  in the library."
- Eureka moments in debugging narratives.
- Character realizations about themselves or others.
- The "aha" moment in problem-solving stories.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Insight, Realization
- Contained by: Scene, Character Arc

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Distinct from Definition
(which explains) and Claim+Evidence (which argues). Epiphany is
experiential—you feel the click.
