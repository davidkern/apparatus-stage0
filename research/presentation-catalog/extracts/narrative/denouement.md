**Definition**: The untying of remaining threads and establishment of new
equilibrium.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to experience closure—
understanding how the world has changed and what the new normal looks like.

**Examples**:
- "A month later, the refactored system had handled three traffic spikes
  without incident."
- The postmortem that establishes lessons learned.
- Characters shown in their new situations after the story.
- Epilogues that show long-term consequences.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Resolution, New Status
- Contained by: Story, Narrative Arc

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Summary and Status but
with closure function—it's not just what is, but what the journey means.
