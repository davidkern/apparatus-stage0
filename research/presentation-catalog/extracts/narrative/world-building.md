**Definition**: Constructing the larger context and rules within which
the narrative operates.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to understand the
world—its constraints, possibilities, and the logic that governs it.

**Examples**:
- Explaining how the deployment pipeline works before describing a
  deployment failure.
- Fantasy novels establishing magic systems.
- Technical architecture overviews that set up debugging narratives.
- The rules of the game before the game is played.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Rules, Constraints, Possibilities
- Contained by: Story, Exposition

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Definition and
Explanation but for systems rather than concepts. World-building creates
the possibility space.
