**Definition**: Establishing the spatial, temporal, and atmospheric
context for action.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to be located—to
know where and when events occur and what the environment feels like.

**Examples**:
- "It was 2 AM in the data center, server fans humming."
- Establishing shots in film.
- The description of a meeting room before the meeting begins.
- Atmospheric openings in fiction.

**Containment**:
- Contains: Location, Time, Atmosphere
- Contained by: Scene

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Status but spatial
and atmospheric rather than evaluative. Setting locates; Status assesses.
