## Observations

### "Emotional" appears to be a catch-all

The Emotional category (11 items) contains items that seem functionally distinct:

**Plausibly emotional:**
- empathy — acknowledging audience's feelings
- reassurance — alleviating anxiety
- urgency — creating sense of immediacy

**Seem more argumentative:**
- anticipation — addressing objections (classical prolepsis)
- refutation — dismantling contrary position
- analogy — explaining via comparison
- maxim — general principle/wisdom

**Seem more structural:**
- framing — setting context (classical narratio)
- attention-capture — opening move
- recapitulation — closing summary (classical peroration)
- timeliness — temporal relevance (classical kairos)

### Possible data quality issue

The heterogeneity of "Emotional" may indicate the extraction agent:
- Ran out of category ideas and used Emotional as default
- Had a different understanding of "emotional" than expected
- Was working from source material that grouped these together
