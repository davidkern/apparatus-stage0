**Definition**: Linguistic softening that reduces the impact of an inherently
face-threatening act (request, criticism, disagreement).

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to perform a necessary action (request,
correct, disagree) while minimizing damage to the hearer's positive or
negative face.

**Examples**:
- "I hate to ask, but could you possibly..." (mitigated request)
- "You might want to consider..." (softened suggestion)
- "I could be wrong, but..." (hedged disagreement)
- "Sorry to bother you..." (pre-apology for intrusion)
- Passive voice to avoid naming agent: "Mistakes were made"

**Containment**:
- Contains: Hedge, Apology, Request
- Contained by: Turn

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to hedging in Evidential
(Confidence, Uncertainty) but focused on social face rather than epistemic
state. The uncertainty may be performed rather than genuine.
