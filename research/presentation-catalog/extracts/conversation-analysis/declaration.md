**Definition**: Utterance that changes institutional or social reality by
being uttered.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to bring about a new state of affairs
through the performative power of the utterance itself.

**Examples**:
- "You're hired" / "You're fired"
- "I pronounce this version 2.0"
- "This feature is now deprecated"
- "Meeting adjourned"
- "I resign"
- "The issue is closed"

**Containment**:
- Contains: (the performative)
- Contained by: (institutional context)

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Status (State) but
performative rather than descriptive. Status reports current state;
Declaration changes it.
