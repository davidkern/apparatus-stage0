**Definition**: A specific type of backchannel that invites the speaker to
continue their extended turn.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to yield the floor back to the current
speaker, signaling receptivity to more content.

**Examples**:
- "Uh-huh" (with continuing intonation)
- "And then?"
- "Go on..."
- "Tell me more"
- Ellipsis in written chat indicating expectation of more

**Containment**:
- Contains: (minimal)
- Contained by: Turn

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Specialized form of Backchannel.
Distinct because it actively encourages continuation rather than just
acknowledging.
