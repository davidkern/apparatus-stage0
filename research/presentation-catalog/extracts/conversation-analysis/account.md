**Definition**: Explanation provided with or for a dispreferred response.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to justify a face-threatening response
(refusal, disagreement) by providing reasons.

**Examples**:
- "I can't make it because I have another commitment"
- "I disagree--here's why..."
- Explanation following a "no"
- "The reason I can't is..."

**Containment**:
- Contains: Excuse, Justification, Explanation
- Contained by: Dispreferred response

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Container for Excuse/Justification.
The obligation to account is a structural feature of dispreferred responses.
