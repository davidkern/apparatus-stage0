**Definition**: Deliberately obvious violation of a conversational maxim to
convey implicature.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to communicate something through the
recognizable violation of expected cooperative behavior.

**Examples**:
- Obvious sarcasm (violating quality maxim)
- Answering with excessive irrelevance (violating relation maxim)
- Giving obviously incomplete information (violating quantity maxim)
- Being deliberately obscure (violating manner maxim)
- Overstatement and understatement

**Containment**:
- Contains: (the flouting mechanism)
- Contained by: Turn

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: No direct analog. Meta-communicative
through deliberate norm violation.
