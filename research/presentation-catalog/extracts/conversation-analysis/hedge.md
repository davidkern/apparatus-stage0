**Definition**: Linguistic marker that reduces commitment, certainty, or
force of a statement.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to leave room for disagreement, avoid
overprecision, or soften impact through vagueness or qualification.

**Examples**:
- "I think...", "maybe", "sort of", "kind of"
- "It seems like...", "apparently", "arguably"
- "In my opinion...", "from my perspective"
- "Roughly speaking...", "more or less"
- "If I'm not mistaken..."

**Containment**:
- Contains: (qualification markers)
- Contained by: Any assertive statement

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Uncertainty/Confidence
(Evidential) but broader function. Hedges can express genuine uncertainty
OR perform politeness regardless of actual certainty.
