**Definition**: Utterance that validates, appreciates, or shows interest in
the hearer.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to make the hearer feel valued, liked,
or included--enhancing their positive face needs.

**Examples**:
- "Great question!"
- "That's a really interesting point"
- "I love how you approached this"
- Compliments on work or ideas
- Using inclusive "we" rather than distancing "you"
- Showing interest: "Tell me more about that"

**Containment**:
- Contains: Praise, Acknowledgement
- Contained by: Turn, Feedback

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Praise (Persuasive from
Aristotle) but focused on social maintenance rather than evaluation. Could
also relate to Confidence-Building (Motivation from Instructional Design).
