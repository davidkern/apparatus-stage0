**Definition**: Utterance that commits the speaker to a future course of action.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to bind themselves to do something,
creating an obligation.

**Examples**:
- Promises: "I'll have it done by Friday"
- Offers: "Let me handle that"
- Vows: "I commit to maintaining this"
- Threats: "If you do X, I will do Y"
- Contracts and agreements

**Containment**:
- Contains: Future action description
- Contained by: Sequence

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Command/Step (Instruction)
but self-directed. The direction of obligation is toward speaker rather than
hearer. Partially covered by Error Handling (Suggestion as future commitment
to fix).
