**Definition**: Brief response that signals attention, understanding, or
engagement without claiming a full conversational turn.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to indicate "I'm listening, continue"
without interrupting or shifting to speaker role. Shows active recipiency.

**Examples**:
- "Uh-huh", "mm-hmm", "yeah"
- "Right", "okay", "I see"
- Nodding (in multimodal contexts)
- "Interesting", "wow", "really?"
- Typing indicators in chat
- Short affirmations: "Makes sense", "Got it"

**Containment**:
- Contains: (minimal)
- Contained by: Turn (as listener contribution)

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Acknowledgement (Emphasis)
but lighter. Acknowledgement confirms receipt of specific input; Backchannel
signals ongoing attention. Backchannel is meta-communicative about the channel
itself.
