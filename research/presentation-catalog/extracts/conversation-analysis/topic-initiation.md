**Definition**: Introduction of a new conversational topic.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to establish a new focus for the
conversation, typically by making a topic available and checking recipient
uptake.

**Examples**:
- "Did you hear about...?"
- "Speaking of which..."
- "Have you seen the latest on X?"
- News announcements: "Guess what happened"
- Elicitors: "What's new?"

**Containment**:
- Contains: (topic carrier)
- Contained by: Turn

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Marker (Transition) but
specifically about topic introduction rather than generic boundary.
