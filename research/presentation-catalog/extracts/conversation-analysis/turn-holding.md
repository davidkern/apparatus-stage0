name: Turn-Holding
---

**Definition**: Signal that the current speaker intends to continue despite
natural pause or completion points.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to maintain the floor to complete a
multi-unit turn without interruption.

**Examples**:
- "and um..." (filled pause)
- "wait, there's more"
- "hold on, I'm not done"
- Rising intonation suggesting continuation
- List intonation (non-final items)
- "Three things: first..." (projecting multi-part structure)

**Containment**:
- Contains: (continuation markers)
- Contained by: Turn (extended)

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: No direct analog. Opposite of
Turn-Yielding. Meta-communicative about discourse structure.
