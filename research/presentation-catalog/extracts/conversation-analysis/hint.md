**Definition**: Utterance that indirectly conveys meaning by relying on
contextual inference rather than explicit statement.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to communicate something without
saying it directly, preserving deniability or avoiding imposition.

**Examples**:
- Mentioning a problem without explicitly requesting help
- "Nice code you have there" (implying criticism through marked praise)
- "The tests are taking a while to run" (implying concern about complexity)
- Leaving relevant information conspicuously absent
- "Some people might think that's an issue..."

**Containment**:
- Contains: (the hint carrier)
- Contained by: Turn

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Distinct from explicit forms. Related
to instructional Hint (Guidance from Instructional Design) but that is about
graduated support; this is about social indirection.
