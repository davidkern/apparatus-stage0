**Definition**: Signal that moves conversation from one topic to another.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to change the conversational focus,
either stepwise (gradual) or boundaried (explicit).

**Examples**:
- "Anyway..." (boundaried shift)
- "That reminds me..." (stepwise connection)
- "On a different note..."
- "Before I forget..."
- "Oh, I meant to tell you..."

**Containment**:
- Contains: Marker, Topic-Initiation
- Contained by: Turn

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Close to Marker (Transition) but
specifically about topic structure rather than generic structure.
