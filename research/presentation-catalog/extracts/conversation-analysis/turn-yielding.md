**Definition**: Signal that the current speaker is completing their turn and
making the floor available.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to hand off the conversational floor
to another participant.

**Examples**:
- Falling intonation at end of statement
- "What do you think?"
- "Over to you"
- "Does that make sense?"
- Explicit addressee selection: "John?"
- Completion of adjacency pair first part (question expects answer)

**Containment**:
- Contains: (varies)
- Contained by: Turn

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Marker (Transition) but
specifically about turn management rather than topic structure.
