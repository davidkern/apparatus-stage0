**Definition**: Explanation that admits the wrongfulness of an act but denies
full responsibility.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to mitigate blame by explaining
external factors that contributed to the offense.

**Examples**:
- "The server was down, so I couldn't submit in time"
- "I didn't receive the notification"
- "I wasn't aware that was a requirement"
- "The documentation wasn't clear"
- "There were circumstances beyond my control"

**Containment**:
- Contains: Causation (external attribution), Apology (often partial)
- Contained by: Remedial interchange

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Defense (Persuasive from
Aristotle) but specifically about responsibility mitigation. Related to
Explanation but with exculpatory purpose.
