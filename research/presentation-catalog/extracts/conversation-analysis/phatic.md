**Definition**: Utterance whose primary purpose is establishing or maintaining
social connection rather than conveying propositional content.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to signal social presence, openness to
interaction, and relational warmth. Malinowski's "phatic communion" describes
language used to create "ties of union" through the mere exchange of words.

**Examples**:
- "How's it going?" (not expecting detailed answer)
- "Nice weather we're having."
- "Happy Friday!"
- Status messages in chat: "Good morning team!"
- Slack emoji reactions that signal acknowledgment without content
- "Hey" as conversation opener

**Containment**:
- Contains: (minimal propositional content)
- Contained by: Session, Turn

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Greeting/Farewell (Transition)
but can occur throughout interaction, not just at boundaries. Greeting is about
ritual brackets; Phatic is about social bonding at any point.
