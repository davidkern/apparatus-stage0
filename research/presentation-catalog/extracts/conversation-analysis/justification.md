**Definition**: Explanation that admits responsibility but denies the
wrongfulness of the act.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to reframe the apparent offense as
actually appropriate given the circumstances.

**Examples**:
- "I had to break the API because the security fix required it"
- "The delay was necessary to ensure quality"
- "I prioritized the critical bug over the feature request"
- "Sometimes you have to break things to make progress"
- "It was the lesser of two evils"

**Containment**:
- Contains: Claim+Evidence, Causation
- Contained by: Remedial interchange

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Very close to Defense (Persuasive
from Aristotle). May be identical or Defense could be seen as broader
container.
