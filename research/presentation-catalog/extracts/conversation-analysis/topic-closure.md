**Definition**: Signal that a topic is being wound down or terminated.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to end discussion of the current topic,
making space for shift or termination.

**Examples**:
- "So, anyway..." (summary + transition)
- "Right, so that's that"
- "Well, I think we've covered that"
- Assessment tokens: "Good", "Fine", "Right"
- Summarizing before moving on

**Containment**:
- Contains: Summary (often), Marker
- Contained by: Turn, Section

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Summary (Transition)
when summary is used for closure. Also related to Farewell (for
conversation-level closure).
