**Definition**: Prospective statement that anticipates and preempts potential
negative evaluation.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants to protect face before an action by
signaling awareness of potential problems.

**Examples**:
- "I haven't tested this, but..."
- "This is just a rough draft"
- "Off the top of my head..."
- "I'm still learning this, but..."
- "This might be controversial..."
- "Devil's advocate here..."

**Containment**:
- Contains: Self-Deprecation (often), Hedge
- Contained by: Pre-sequence to claim or action

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Related to Anticipation (Argumentative
from Aristotle) but about self-protection rather than objection-handling.
Prospective face-work.

---


Conversation analysis reveals that responses to invitations, requests, and
offers are structurally marked as "preferred" (acceptance) or "dispreferred"
(refusal). This marking is about social solidarity.
