**Definition**: Communicating that diligent effort with consideration of
one's own interests is required.

**Semantic Purpose**: The author wants the audience to understand that
effort is required but balanced against other legitimate concerns.

**Legal Formalization**:
- **reasonable efforts**: "Shall use reasonable efforts"
- **commercially reasonable efforts**: Business-context variant
- **reasonable endeavours** (UK): British equivalent

**Informal Appearance**:
- "Try to..." (moderate effort expectation)
- "See what you can do..." (effort without absolute commitment)
- "Do your best within reason..." (balanced effort)

**Ambiguity Resolved**: Reasonable efforts allows party to consider costs,
feasibility, and competing priorities. It's effort "within reason."

**Containment**:
- Contains: Effort standard, Target outcome, Balancing factors
- Contained by: Obligation, Covenant

**Relationship to Existing Categories**: Moderate version of Best-Efforts.
These form a spectrum of effort-level intents.

**Forms**:
- *Typographic*: Standard effort qualifier
- *Auditory*: Moderate emphasis
- *Visual*: Standard commitment level
